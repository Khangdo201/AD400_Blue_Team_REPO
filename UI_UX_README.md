# CareerCompare — UI/UX Design

Designer: Fathiya Yoosef

## Purpose

CareerCompare helps users compare job offers based on compensation,
benefits, and personal priorities. These designs guide the team's
frontend implementation.

## FigJam Board

[View CareerCompare designs in FigJam](https://www.figma.com/board/8biN2Z9cTvQZV51K6lmMBK/Wireframe?node-id=0-1).

The board includes:
- User persona
- User journey map
- Low-fidelity wireframes
- High-fidelity mockups
- Color palette

## Primary Persona: Alex

- Age: 22
- Role: Recent technology graduate seeking a first software development job
- Location: Seattle
- Technical skill: High; comfortable using technology but inexperienced
  in evaluating job offers
- Devices: Laptop and mobile phone

### Bio

Alex is a 22-year-old technology graduate in Seattle looking for a
first job in the industry. With limited experience evaluating job
offers, Alex needs help comparing salary, bonuses, benefits, and
learning opportunities. Alex wants to choose a role that supports
career growth and matches personal priorities.

### Goals

- Easily compare salary, bonuses, equity, and benefits.
- Find opportunities for learning and career growth.
- Choose an offer that matches personal priorities.

### Frustrations

- Offer information is scattered across emails and documents.
- Equity and vesting terms can be difficult to understand.
- Comparing several offers can feel overwhelming.

### Needs

- A clean interface that works on laptops and phones.
- Clear side-by-side comparisons.
- Plain-language labels and explanations.
- A way to set personal priorities.
- Clear identification of missing offer information.

### User Scenario

Alex enters two job offers on a laptop at home. Later, Alex reviews
the comparison on a phone at a café and identifies the offer that
best matches their salary and career growth priorities.

## User Journey

1. Receive two job offers.
2. Enter the details into CareerCompare.
3. Set personal priorities.
4. Compare the offers side by side.
5. Identify the offer that best supports personal goals.

The journey map shows Alex's actions, thoughts, and feelings.
It is a proposed journey based on the persona.

## Screen Designs

| Screen | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Introduce the app and provide access to key actions |
| My Offers | List saved offers and select two for comparison |
| Add Job Offer | Enter compensation and benefit details |
| Offer Details | Review a saved offer |
| My Priorities | Set the importance of comparison factors |
| Compare Offers | Review two offers side by side |

## Design Guidance

- Use the FigJam color palette consistently.
- Keep navigation, buttons, and form labels consistent.
- Display "Not provided" when optional offer details are missing.
- Include an empty state prompting users to add their first offer.
- Support laptop and mobile layouts.
- Use readable text and clear contrast.
- Show validation messages beside the relevant form fields.

The mockups describe proposed appearance and behavior.
They do not represent completed application functionality.
